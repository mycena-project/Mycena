Mycelium Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

Mycelium is designed to collect as little data as possible.

  • Your device: A cryptographic identity key pair is generated and stored locally on your device. This never leaves your device.
  • Messages: Messages are stored locally on your device with an expiry date. They are transmitted through the Mycelium network in encrypted form. We cannot read your messages.
  • The relay server: When connecting through the internet relay, your IP address is temporarily processed to establish the connection. No IP addresses are logged or stored.

What we do NOT collect

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No message content (all messages are end-to-end encrypted)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No advertising identifiers

Data on your device

You can delete all local data at any time using the "Panic Wipe" function in Settings. This permanently deletes all messages, your identity key, and all app data.
