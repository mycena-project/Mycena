Emergency local credits (MeshCoin preview)

Mycena includes bounded offline emergency credits (MXC units) for neighborhood commerce during outages — similar to prepaid utility credits, not money or an investment. Balances and transfers are capped by the app. There is no expectation of profit, no market price, and no guarantee of value. Witness-based settlement is experimental and has not been independently audited. Do not use emergency credits for savings, speculation, or high-value transactions.
