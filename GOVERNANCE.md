# Governance

Mycena is an independent, non-commercial open-source project.

- **Maintainer:** Alex (Alexander Senf). The maintainer reviews and merges
  changes, sets the roadmap and makes final decisions.
- **Decisions** are made in public: issues and pull requests on GitHub, with
  larger design choices recorded as ADRs in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/).
- **Contributors** who repeatedly make good contributions can be invited as
  co-maintainers. The goal is a project that does not depend on one person.
- **Security reports** go through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Licensing:** AGPL-3.0-or-later, with a Contributor License Agreement
  ([CLA.md](CLA.md)) so the project can also be offered under other licenses
  to sustain its development. The AGPL version always remains available.
- **Funding:** the project may accept grants and donations. Funders do not
  receive control over the roadmap or the code.
